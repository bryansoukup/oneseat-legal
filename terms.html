The product

OneSeat is an editorial guide to restaurants that work for a party of one. It is not a reservation system, not a safety certification, and not a statement about who a restaurant welcomes.

Solo Fit

Chair grades reflect available evidence that a place works for dining alone at the hour you picked: seating, party-of-one booking, walk-in notes, published hours, and similar signals. They are not food ratings. Paid placement cannot change a grade.

Hours and booking

Hours, kitchen schedules, and booking rules change. Confirm with the restaurant before you go. Booking happens on the restaurant’s own site or on OpenTable, Resy, or Tock.

New York is free

Other cities unlock with a one-time In-App Purchase handled by Apple. Restore purchases on the same Apple ID from the Guide.

Shared Tables
